Data & export

Download your data or bring it in from a file.

Tickets CSV has one row per sale (rows with the same lot share one group of tickets): lot, event, event_date, qty_bought, cost_per_ticket, sector, row, seats, transferred, notes, sale_date, qty_sold, price_per_ticket. Pulling CSV: date, client, event, qty, profit, sector, row, seats, transferred, notes, event_date. For tickets, the transferred column accepts YES, PENDING or NO. Import detects the type from the header. A JSON backup ({lots, pulls}) replaces all data.
